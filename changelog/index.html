Changelog

What’s new in Trkly, newest first. Trkly is in early testing, so things change quickly.

20 Nov 2026

Works with no signal

Trkly now keeps going when your phone doesn’t have signal. Add and edit expenses anywhere, and they send themselves when you’re back online.

  • Offline adding and editing. On a ferry, in the outback or abroad without data, add and change expenses as usual. They wait on your phone and send automatically when signal returns.
  • Opens straight away. Your trips and their expenses open instantly from the copy on your phone, then update when there’s signal.
  • You can see what’s waiting. A notice at the top says you’re offline and how many expenses are waiting, and each one is marked until it has gone to the group.
  • Stays signed in. Opening Trkly with no signal no longer sends you back to the sign-in screen.
  • Two-step sign-in is now optional. You no longer need an authenticator app to start a trip. You can still turn it on in Account for extra security, and Trkly suggests it once on the home screen.

Deleting an expense and reading a receipt still need a connection.

19 Nov 2026

Home screen, and your data on your terms

Put Trkly on your home screen in a couple of taps, and delete a trip or your account whenever you like.

  • Add to home screen. Right after you join a trip, Trkly offers to add itself to your home screen so it opens like an app. On iPhone it shows the two steps; on Android it’s one tap.
  • Delete a trip. Organisers can delete a trip for everyone, including its expenses, payments and receipt photos, from the Organiser tab.
  • Delete your account. In Account, delete your sign-in and details. Expenses you added stay on shared trips as “Former member” so everyone else’s totals still add up.
  • Help is one tap away. The Account screen now shows help@trkly.app.
18 Nov 2026

A privacy notice

A plain-English page explaining what Trkly keeps, who can see it, and how we measure how the app is used.

  • Privacy page. Find it at trkly.app/privacy, linked from the sign-in screen and the bottom of every page.
  • Usage measurement. Trkly now records which features are used and which errors people hit, so we can fix problems faster. It’s linked to a random ID, not your name or email, and we never sell it.
17 Nov 2026

Who changed what, and your usuals

See everything that’s happened on the trip, log your regular spots in one tap, and take the numbers away in a spreadsheet.

  • Activity. A new tab shows who added, changed or deleted what, plus payments and who joined, newest first.
  • History on every expense. Open an expense to see who added it and exactly what changed since: “amount A$84.60 → A$86.40 · paid by you → Tom”.
  • Your usuals. The places you go most appear as one-tap shortcuts with the usual amount and split. They follow the time of day, so the morning café comes first at breakfast.
  • Download as a spreadsheet. Get every expense as a CSV file for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, with each person’s share and totals.
  • Made for iPad and laptop. On bigger screens your balance sits beside the expense list, and Add expense is always at the top.
  • Once the organiser closes the trip for settling up, expenses can no longer be changed or deleted.
17 Nov 2026

Scan your receipts

Take a photo of a receipt and Trkly fills in the expense for you. You check it, then save.

  • Receipt scanning. The amount, currency, date, place and category are read from the photo and highlighted so you can check them. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.
  • The photo stays with the expense, so anyone on the trip can open it if a number looks wrong.
  • One receipt per photo works best. If a photo has several, Trkly asks you to take them one at a time.
  • Join with a QR code. Together in person? Organisers can show a QR code that people scan to join the trip, or download it to print or share.
  • Invite-only for now. While Trkly is in early testing, organisers add the email addresses of the people they invite.
16 Nov 2026

Settling up, properly

The end of a trip is where money gets awkward. Settle up now handles the real-world details.

  • Rates fixed at the end. When the organiser closes the trip, exchange rates are fixed so everyone sees the same numbers in their own currency.
  • Record what you paid, including part payments. The person you paid confirms it, and either of you can undo a mistake.
  • Nudge people who still owe, with a friendly reminder message you can send from your own phone.
  • Payment details made simple. Add up to three ways to pay you (bank, Venmo, Revolut and more) and pick your favourite.
  • Organisers can edit the trip after creating it: name, dates and more.
  • Clearer messages when something goes wrong, written in plain English, and the app now updates itself quietly in the background.
16 Nov 2026

Quicker every day

Lots of small changes so logging a coffee takes seconds.

  • Dates read like a person would say them: Today, Yesterday, Sat 20 Dec.
  • One-tap categories, and Again? buttons to repeat the things you buy every day.
  • When you create a trip, Trkly helps you invite the group straight away: WhatsApp, Messages or a link.
  • Organisers sign in with Google or Apple and confirm with a code on their phone, since they control the trip.
  • Choosing an end date now opens on the right month.
15 Nov 2026

Hello, Trkly

The first version of Trkly, built for one family trip to Australia.

  • Create a trip and invite everyone with a link. No passwords: you sign in with a link sent to your email.
  • Group people into households, so a family can pay and be paid as one.
  • Split costs equally, by household, by shares or by exact amounts, and leave out anyone who wasn't there.
  • Spend in any currency. Each person sees what they owe or are owed in their own money.
  • Settle up with the fewest payments possible.
