Privacy

How Trkly handles your information. Last updated 18 November 2026.

In short

The short version

  • No ads, and we never sell your data. Your information is used to run Trkly and make it better, nothing else.
  • Trkly never moves money. It works out who owes whom. You pay each other in your own apps, and we never see bank or card details.
  • Your trip is shared only with the people on it.
  • We don’t browse your trips. We only look at trip details when you ask us for help, or when we have to in order to fix a problem or keep Trkly secure.
  • We measure how the app is used (see Product analytics and diagnostics below) so we can fix errors and improve it.
What we collect

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, and the sign-in method you use (an email link, Apple or Google). If you’re an organiser, there’s also the fact that two-step sign-in is turned on. We never see your authenticator codes.
  • Your profile. Your display name, home currency, and the payment details you choose to add (for example a Venmo username), so others know how to pay you.
  • Your trips. Trip names and dates, who is on the trip and in which household, expenses (amount, currency, date, place, category, notes, how they’re split), payments marked as made, and a history of changes.
  • Receipt photos. These are stored only if you take or add one.
Analytics

Product analytics and diagnostics

We collect product usage and diagnostic data to keep the app reliable, fix errors and improve features. This includes which features are used, which screens people visit, error messages shown in the app, performance information, and device, browser and operating system details.

  • This data is linked to a random account ID, never to your email address or name. Some of it we only look at in aggregate, across everyone.
  • Screen recordings. Where they’re turned on, we may record how the app’s screens are used, to understand where people get stuck. Anything you type is hidden in these recordings, and receipt photos are never included.
  • Before anything is sent, we remove the parts of web addresses that identify a particular trip, invite link or sign-in link.
  • Service providers process this data on our behalf. We keep it only as long as we need it for these purposes.
Who sees what

Who can see your information

  • People on your trip see the trip’s expenses, balances, payments, the activity history, each other’s display names and households, and any payment details people have chosen to add.
  • Organisers can also see the email addresses they’ve invited, and can remove people from the trip.
  • The Trkly team can technically reach the stored data, as any app’s operators can. We don’t look at your trips except to help when you ask, or to fix a problem or keep Trkly secure.
Service providers

Companies that help run Trkly

These companies process data only to provide their service to us.

  • Supabase stores accounts, trips and receipt photos, and handles sign-in.
  • Cloudflare hosts the app and this website.
  • Anthropic reads receipt photos when you tap Scan receipt, to pre-fill the expense. Nothing is saved until you check it and tap Save.
  • PostHog handles product analytics and diagnostics.
  • Apple and Google sign you in, if you choose Continue with Apple or Google.
  • Google Forms collects beta sign-up requests and tester feedback.
Your choices

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Trip information is kept for as long as the trip exists, so everyone can see it and settle up. A deleted expense no longer counts, but the trip’s history still shows that it was deleted.
  • To get a copy of your information, correct it, or have your account and data deleted, email us at the address below. We’ll do it and confirm. Expenses you added to a shared trip may stay with the trip, without your name, so the totals still add up for everyone else.
  • Trkly is for people aged 13 and over.
