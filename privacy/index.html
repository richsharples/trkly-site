Privacy

Plain English, no small print. Last updated 17 November 2026.

The short version

  • Trkly stores what it needs to split costs on your trip, and nothing else.
  • Only people on your trip can see your trip’s expenses, receipts and payment details.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell or share your data.
  • Trkly never moves money. It only shows who owes whom.
  • Ask us at any time and we’ll delete your data.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, and how you sign in (an email link, Google or Apple). Trip organisers also set up a two-step sign-in code.
  • Your details: the name, home currency and household you choose, and any payment details you add so people can pay you back (for example a Venmo name or bank details). Only add what you’re happy for your trip to see.
  • Your trips: the expenses you and others log (amount, place, date, category, who paid, how it’s split, notes), payments you record, and a log of who added or changed what.
  • Receipt photos, if you take them. The app shrinks each photo and removes its location data before uploading it.

Who can see it

Everyone on a trip can see that trip’s expenses, receipts, activity, and the names and payment details of the people on it. People who aren’t on your trip can’t. The organiser can also see who has been invited.

Who helps us run Trkly

A few services do work for us. They only get what they need for that job:

  • Supabase stores the data and handles sign-in. Our database is hosted in Canada.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and app, and runs the receipt reader.
  • Resend sends your sign-in emails.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads a receipt photo when you tap Scan receipt, and sends back the details. It gets the photo and the trip’s currency and dates, nothing else.
  • Frankfurter supplies exchange rates. It doesn’t get any of your data.

Cookies and tracking

Trkly doesn’t use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser keeps your sign-in and a few settings (like the split you last used) on your device so you don’t have to repeat them.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and your details, or a whole trip, send us a request with the email you sign in with. We’ll do it within 30 days and confirm when it’s done. Because a trip’s numbers depend on everyone’s expenses, deleting one person from a trip that others are still settling may wait until the trip is settled, or we’ll replace your name with “Former member”.

Trkly is in beta

Trkly is a small, independent project being tested with a small group of families and friends. If this note changes, we’ll update the date above and mention it in the changelog. Questions? Get in touch.
