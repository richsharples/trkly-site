New · Receipt scanning

Snap the receipt.
That’s the expense.

Take a photo and Trkly reads the place, the total, the currency and the date, and picks a category. What it read is highlighted so you can check it at a glance. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.

  • Picks the amount you actually paid, tips and service charge included, not the subtotal
  • Reads receipts from anywhere, in any currency
  • The photo stays with the expense, so anyone on the trip can check a number

Three steps, then back to the beach.

01

Share one link

Send the invite to the family chat, or show a QR code when you’re together. Everyone picks their household and home currency, and they’re in.

02

Log it as you go

Snap the receipt, tap one of your usuals, or type the amount. Split with everyone, by household, by shares or exact amounts.

03

Settle up once

At the end, Trkly shows the fewest payments, each in the payer’s own currency, with the payee’s Venmo, bank or Wise details.

Small things that stop the arguments.

Your usuals

The morning café, the corner shop. Your regular spots appear as one-tap shortcuts with the usual amount and split, and they follow the time of day.

Households

Split by family, and settle one payment per household instead of a dozen.

Who changed what

Every add, edit and delete is in the activity feed, and each expense shows its own history. No “who changed the dinner bill?”

Join with a QR code

Together at the airport? The organiser shows a code, everyone scans it, and the group is set up in a minute.

Take the numbers with you

Download every expense as a spreadsheet for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets, with each person’s share and totals.

Phone, iPad or laptop

Log on your phone at the table, review on the iPad back at the apartment. Same trip, everywhere.

Everyone sees their own money.

Spend in the local currency, think in your own. Each expense locks the day’s exchange rate, and you can change the rate if your card charged differently. When the organiser closes the trip, rates are fixed so everyone sees the same numbers.
